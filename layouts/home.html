Sunrise

Install Sunrise → View source ↗

Free & open source · Runs locally · Bring your own game files

01 / ABOUT

Bringing content back from the grave.

Sunrise installs onto an old build of Destiny 2 and lets you experience all its content once again, without requiring a server. It is designed to visit old destinations, replay old campaigns and experience the state of the game before sunsetting, not as a replacement for the live game.

The project is open source and does not contain any game data. Some features are WIP or not present yet (most missions, p2p multiplayer, full progression, ...)
